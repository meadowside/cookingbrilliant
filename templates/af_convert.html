What real packs do

  • The air fryer time was between {{ cmp.pct_lo }}% and {{ cmp.pct_hi }}% of the oven time. Half of them fell between {{ cmp.pct_q1 }}% and {{ cmp.pct_q3 }}%, and the middle value was {{ cmp.pct_med }}%, so a typical pack cuts the time by about {{ 100 - cmp.pct_med }}%.
  • Against the fan oven setting, {{ cmp.same_temp }} packs used the same temperature in the air fryer, {{ cmp.lower }} went lower and {{ cmp.higher }} went higher. The differences ran from {{ cmp.diff_lo | abs }}°C lower to {{ cmp.diff_hi }}°C higher.
