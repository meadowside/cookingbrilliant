    How the rounding works

    Grams and millilitres over 100 round to the nearest 5. Smaller amounts round to the nearest gram, or half a gram under 10g. Cups and spoons round to the nearest quarter, or third where that's closer. Eggs and other things you count get a fraction plus a plain-English suggestion, since nobody can crack a third of an egg.

    Lines without a number at the start (like "salt to taste") are left as they are. Baking times and tin sizes don't scale in a straight line, so check those yourself.
